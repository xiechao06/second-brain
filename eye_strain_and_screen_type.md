2. Blue light from digital devices, such as computers, phones, and tablets, is often blamed for causing eye damage and discomfort. However, the American Academy of Ophthalmology says there is no scientific evidence that blue light from screens is harmful to the eye. The main cause of eye strain and dryness from screen time is digital eye strain, which can be prevented by taking regular breaks, blinking more, and using artificial tears. The Academy also advises to limit screen time before bed and to use nighttime settings on devices to reduce blue light exposure. Blue light is not the only source of light that affects the eye. Sunlight and fluorescent light can also cause eye problems, such as cataracts, myopia, and cancer. The Academy warns that taking preventive measures against blue light could have unintended consequences and that more research is needed to understand its effects.
