# Are some kinds of knowledge more valuable in nature?

Here I define knowledge as **knowing facts**, "in nature" I mean in all time and locations.
Short answer: Yes and No.

## Yes part

Compare the knowledge of *Abstract algebra* and the knowledge of *cooking*.

* *Abstract algebra* is much harder.
* *Abstract algebra* gives your brain more exercises.
* *Abstract algebra* helps to tackle the frontier of science and technology.
* *Abstract algebra* gives you a higher vision of world.

## No part

For a cook, "the frontier of science and technology" is **meaningless**, knowledge of *cooking* is what he needs most to **get job done**.

    The knowledge that gets your job done is more valuable.

But it is not that simple, first you must clarify your tasks into 2 kinds:

* short term tasks
* long term tasks

For a cook, the knowledge of *nutrition* provides great long term value to the task: **BECOME A MASTER COOK**.
