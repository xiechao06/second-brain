# Get My Job Done

## Abstract

* Define your **visible output** well and revise it repeatedly.
* Focus the **visible output**.
* Make time limitation.
* Get a bird view, BFS rather than DFS.
* You may come across some curious ideas or knowledge, and they may not relevant to the output, you could put them into memo and handle them later. That is, create some seperation between **learning** and **project**.
